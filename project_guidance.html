About Service

Building an embedded project for your final year, a hackathon, or a personal portfolio? I've mentored 20+ students and professionals through end-to-end project builds — from idea selection to architecture design to final demo prep.

This session is a deep-dive into your project. Whether you're stuck on a tricky bug, need help choosing the right architecture, or want an expert review of your design — I'll help you move forward with confidence.

  • Architecture & Design Review: Evaluate your hardware/software architecture — MCU selection, peripheral interfaces (SPI, I2C, UART), RTOS task design, and memory layout strategies.
  • Code Review & Debugging: Walk through your C/C++ code together — identify bugs, optimize performance, and apply MISRA-C best practices for safety-critical or production-grade code.
  • Protocol & Integration Help: Guidance on CAN bus communication, UDS diagnostic implementation, AUTOSAR layered architecture, and secure boot / authentication flows.
  • Project Scoping & Roadmap: If you're just starting out, I'll help you define a realistic scope, break it into milestones, and create a timeline that works for your deadline.
  • Demo & Documentation Prep: Tips on structuring your project report, creating effective demo videos, and presenting your work to panels or interviewers.
